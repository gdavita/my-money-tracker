Check actual bank balances

After recording transactions, enter the balance shown by your bank or wallet. The app compares it with your calculated balance. This does not create a transaction or change your records.

Rules to preserve

  • Internal transfers are not expenses or income.
  • Credit-card purchases are expenses; card payment is a liability settlement.
  • Foreign currency purchase is conversion, not spending.
  • Investments are assets, not available spending money.
  • Reimbursements and friends' repayments are not income.

Export / restore
